Bikes, performance parts and bikepacking gear.

Everything we stock is something we'd ride ourselves. Road, gravel, mountain, e-bikes and kids’ bikes, the parts that make them faster, and the gear that lets you disappear for a few days.

Every bike leaves the store sized, set up and ready for the roads outside the door. Come in and talk it through with us.

01

Road bikes

Endurance and race bikes for the long, quiet roads of the Baixa Segarra.

  • Endurance and race geometry
  • Carbon and aluminium frames
  • Fitted to you before you ride away
02

Gravel bikes

Made for the farm tracks and forest roads that start at our door.

  • Adventure and all-road
  • Clearance for wide tyres
  • Mounts for bags and racks
03

Mountain bikes

From cross-country to trail, for the singletrack of the Prades mountains.

  • Hardtail and full suspension
  • Cross-country and trail
  • Suspension set up for your weight
04

E-bikes

A little help on the climbs, so you can ride further and keep up with anyone.

  • E-road and e-gravel
  • E-mountain bikes
  • Motor and battery servicing in our workshop
05

Kids bikes

From the first balance bike to the first road bike, sized so they love riding.

  • Balance bikes
  • Kids and junior sizes
  • Helmets and accessories
06

Bikepacking

Everything for life on the bike, tested on our own trips.

  • Frame, saddle and handlebar bags
  • Racks and cargo cages
  • Dynamo hubs and lights
  • Lightweight sleep systems
